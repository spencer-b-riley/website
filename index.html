About Spencer

Some people are comfortable in one world. Spencer moves between several: three passports, three languages, two continents, and a habit of getting complete strangers to tell him what they really think.

He grew up in French-speaking Switzerland, came of age racing skis in the Alps, and then crossed the Atlantic to study business in Colorado. Now he is back in Switzerland at the University of St. Gallen, one of Europe's top business schools, chasing the question that has followed him everywhere: why do people choose what they choose?

He doesn't guess at the answer. He goes and asks, again and again, until the pattern shows up.
