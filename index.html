Letter of Motivation

I chose a Master's in Marketing Management because I am fascinated by the "why" behind purchase decisions. The fastest way I have found to get to that "why" is to ask people, so most of my projects have been built on conversations.

Read the full letter

In New Venture Creation at CU Boulder, my team and I had to build a startup from scratch. Our idea kept changing over and over again, and every time it did we went back to customers, more than 500 interviews in the end. Once we landed on MOXY, a refillable fragrance product that keeps any perfume's scent all day, those interviews told us what people spend on perfume in a year and what they would be willing to pay for ours. I took on the finances, sent renders of our prototype to manufacturers for quotes, and reworked the pitch each time a new quote moved our margins. By our final pitch I knew where every figure came from.

I did similar work on a project sponsored by the National Security Agency, where our team ran more than 60 stakeholder interviews and proposed CULPER, a framework to audit AI hiring tools for bias. I also learned to be careful with information that isn't mine to share.

At St. Gallen I am now working on a research project for John Deere, looking at how the brand is perceived against six competitors. Part of it means testing how AI assistants rank tractor brands when a farmer asks them for advice, which is a question I didn't expect to find so interesting. I prepared the cost proposal for the field research across four countries, and I can now tell you that a three day ticket to an Irish farm machinery show costs €45.

What I enjoy most is the people: pitching an idea, building trust quickly, and bringing people along. I grew up in French speaking Switzerland, attended Aiglon College, and studied in the United States, so I build relationships easily across cultures. I like the moment when someone trusts you enough to tell you what they really think, and I'd like to build a career around earning that trust.

If that sounds like someone your team could use, I would be glad to talk.

Spencer Riley
